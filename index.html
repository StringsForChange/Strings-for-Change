Who We Are

About Strings for Change

Our Mission

Strings for Change turns music into measurable community support by raising funds for organizations addressing essential needs such as food access, youth services, and housing support.

What We Do

We are a student-led musical initiative that organizes chamber music performances in public spaces to raise money for local and global causes. By rotating ensembles and donating our earnings each quarter, we use music to make meaningful change in our communities!

What to Expect

Members of Strings for Change can expect a supportive and collaborative environment. New members will be guided through their first busking session by more experienced members, so no prior busking experience is required.

Performances are flexible and typically occur about once or twice per month, depending on availability. Members may perform in different chamber group combinations, collaborate with other musicians, and help raise funds for meaningful community causes.

Members are also encouraged to suggest causes they are passionate about and participate in deciding a charitable cause to donate to every quarter!
